System Overview & Architecture

Empowering Patients Through Continuous Health Insights

HealthTrack is a lightweight, responsive health-tech web application designed to help patients monitor daily symptoms, discover health patterns over time, and visualize weekly health trajectories.

🩺

What is HealthTrack?

HealthTrack enables individuals to keep track of subjective physical experiences—from mild migraines to persistent fatigue—without the overhead of bloated enterprise platforms. It prioritizes simplicity, instant client responsiveness, and clear visual summaries to foster meaningful discussions between patients and clinicians.

  • Granular Logging: 10-point color-calibrated severity scale from subtle discomfort to severe pain.
  • Intelligent Autocomplete: Remembers your past entries for instant reuse and consistency.
  • Longitudinal Filtering: Real-time date range filters to zoom into specific episodes or treatment windows.
⚙️

How It Works Behind the Scenes

HealthTrack is engineered following strict architectural separation of concerns:

01

Input Normalization & Validation

Every submission is validated using pure functions. Symptom descriptions are trimmed, whitespace-collapsed, and lowercase-normalized. All validation errors are returned simultaneously for field-level feedback.

02

Resilient Atomic Storage

Records are persisted to disk using a thread-safe JSON repository. Writes use atomic temporary file swaps (os.replace) guarded by thread locks, ensuring zero corruption during crashes or concurrent calls. An automatic corrupt-file recovery mechanism safeguards uptime.

03

Pure Insights Aggregation

Without heavy AI overhead or predictive guesswork, an internal engine groups records by ISO-8601 calendar weeks (starting on Monday), surfaces top symptoms with deterministic tie-breaking, identifies peak severity, and reports week-over-week trends.
